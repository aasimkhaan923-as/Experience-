Q1: Which sensor operates on the principle of change in electrical resistance with strain?

Key Formulas Reference

Process Signal (4–20 mA conversion):

Current (mA) = 4 + [(PV - LRV) / (URV - LRV)] * 16

Wheatstone Bridge (Balanced Rx):

Rx = (R2 * R3) / R1

Ohm's Law Relationship:

V = I * R | P = V * I

Instrumentation Dictionary

Span:

The algebraic difference between the upper range value (URV) and lower range value (LRV).

Hysteresis:

The maximum difference in output value for a given input when approached from increasing vs decreasing direction.

Transducer:

A device that converts energy from one physical form to an electrical signal.

How-To & Guides Section

How to calibrate a 4–20 mA pressure transmitter?

Connect precision pressure source to input, multimeter in series to loop. Set 0% LRV pressure and adjust Zero screw to read 4.00 mA. Apply 100% URV pressure and adjust Span screw for 20.00 mA. Repeat 2-3 times.
